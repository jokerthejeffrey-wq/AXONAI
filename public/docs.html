Community-built. Cloud-hosted.

AXONAI is developed with help from its community. This chat edition runs pretrained SmolLM2 directly in a Vercel Function. Your computer does not host the model or need to stay online.

First message

A new server instance downloads the quantized model, then generates your reply on its CPU. This may take a few minutes. Later requests can reuse that instance, but the cache is temporary and cold starts can happen again.

What this small model can do

SmolLM2 135M is a compact assistant, not the independent AXONAI student. It can make factual and conversational mistakes. Its capability is limited, and replies are capped to keep requests manageable.

Community training

The separate from-scratch student and its CPU/GPU workers use a persistent training coordinator with a SQLite ledger and durable weight storage. The owner must deploy that service independently and approve uploaded weights. Training uploads are not supported by Vercel chat. Do not use this website URL as the training coordinator.

Privacy

Messages are sent to this site's server function for inference. This application does not save conversations or train on them. Hugging Face supplies model files; it does not receive chat prompts through an inference API. Infrastructure logs and policies still apply.
